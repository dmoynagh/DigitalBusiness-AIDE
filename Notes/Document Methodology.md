Purpose
	To make building, authoring,managing and maintaining documents easier

What
	A component that provides basic  management and content standards to documentation.
	Extends AI platforms and also provides human guides 

Approach & key principles
	Facilitate, not enforce approach
	Documents trigger functionality by content which then applies functionality
	Functionality can be applied in part or in full. Enhance as best able, degrade gracefully
	Schema to help authors shape and define content and allow content to be found and used by consumers
	Apply consistency where wanted and needed
	
	

Key functions
	Versioning
	Document Schema
	Change Management
	Language and best practices
